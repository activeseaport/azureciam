Independent Living Risk Assessment

What is the Independent Living Risk Assessment?

Know Your Score™ begins with the Independent Living Risk Assessment™, which addresses your health, habits, and thoughts or feelings about things that influence long-term health, functioning, and ability to live independently. You will receive an overall score plus individual scores for the ten modifiable health risk factors. Know Your Score may help you identify priorities in making changes that will help you stay healthy, independent, and engaged in the activities that matter most to you.

What will happen to the information you provide?

All information collected is held on a secure electronic server managed by HealthyLifetime Inc. Your information will be automatically analyzed and calculated to provide immediate results. Your information will be shared with an authorized agent of your organization to enhance lifestyle interventions tailored to your needs. if you would like to opt out of sharing this information.
